Mixyn

An AI stylist that builds outfits from the clothes you actually own.

The unique kernel

Your real wardrobe, digitized in seconds, plus an AI stylist that builds a complete look from those exact items: in one click, or around a hint like “this oversized shirt” or “burgundy.”

The core loop: three fallbacks on a stuck morning

Who it's for

You, first. You care about fashion and a versatile, mindfully built wardrobe. Most mornings are easy, but on bad-mood mornings nothing feels right, and today you end up asking your boyfriend to decide.

And anyone who owns more clothes than they wear: people who want to shop less, style what they already have, and stop losing mornings to “I have nothing to wear” in front of a full closet.

Why it matters

A tool for your own daily wardrobe, and practice at the backend skills you want.
structuring robust backend architectures, managing data, and seamlessly integrating AI features

What “working” looks like: the 1-minute demo

  1. Sign up with a real account. The wardrobe lives in a database and is still there after logging out and back in.
  2. Upload a few real clothing photos. The background is removed automatically and each photo becomes a clean digital item.
  3. In the Carousel, swipe through tops, bottoms, and shoes to build an outfit by hand.
  4. Hit Generate with AI, optionally with a hint. A complete look appears, built only from your uploaded items. the aha beat

Boundary: now, later, cut

Now In the proof of concept
  • Real accounts from the start: sign-up and log-in (email + password), with profile, clothes, and outfits stored in a real database
  • Upload clothes with AI background removal
  • Outfit Carousel for tops, bottoms, and shoes, starting from a random combination
  • AI outfit generation from your own items: one click, plus an optional hint field
Later Worth doing, just not now
  • Live weather by city, plus the cold-weather jacket/hat overlay
  • AI auto-tagging of material and season (category and color are in the PoC)
  • Accessories step and themed collections (“office,” “date”)
  • Social: friends, sharing, OutfitCHECK photos, star ratings, monthly leaderboard
  • Outfit calendar and daily streak
  • Pitch extras: Group Vibes, Wardrobe Swap, Travel Mode, Cost-per-Wear, Avatar Mannequin
Cut Out of the demo, with reasons
  • “One month later” time skip: depends on the calendar, social features, and fake history, and none of it proves the styling engine.
  • Social and weather in the PoC: “they can be built later once the core styling engine works.”
