An app for the clothes you already own: put outfits together from your own wardrobe, with an AI stylist to help when you don't know what to wear.

The idea

I love clothes, and I wanted to build an app around them. I found a problem that almost everyone runs into, again and again: you don't know what to wear.

So I made an app where you upload your own clothes and put outfits together from them. If you can't get a look right on your own, an assistant is right there in the outfit screen and suggests how to make it better.

And there is a stylist agent that helps with your style as a whole: it tells you how to improve it, puts together several outfit ideas when you don't know what to wear, and much more.

In the learner's words, edited for language (22 October 2026).

The unique kernel

Your real wardrobe, digitized in seconds, plus an AI stylist that builds a complete look from those exact items: in one click on the outfit screen, or around a request like “this oversized shirt,” “burgundy” or “dinner with friends in the cold.” For bigger questions, a stylist chat knows the same closet: outfits for an occasion, what you haven't worn for a while, what your style is and how to make it better, always with what you own.

The core loop: three fallbacks on a stuck morning

Who it's for

You, first. You care about fashion and a versatile, mindfully built wardrobe. Most mornings are easy, but on bad-mood mornings nothing feels right, and today you end up asking your boyfriend to decide.

And anyone who owns more clothes than they wear: people who want to shop less, style what they already have, and stop losing mornings to “I have nothing to wear” in front of a full closet.

Why it matters

A tool for your own daily wardrobe, and practice at the backend skills you want.

What “working” looks like: the demo

  1. Sign up with a real account. The wardrobe lives in a database and is still there after logging out and back in.
  2. Upload a few real clothing photos. The background is removed automatically, and the AI names each piece: its type and colors.
  3. Create outfit: a random look is already on the canvas. Swipe tops, bottoms, and shoes to build an outfit by hand, and add a layer.
  4. Tap Ask AI, optionally with a request. A complete look appears, built only from your uploaded items. the aha beat
  5. Save it, or Wear it today, and find it in the Outfits tab and the Calendar.
  6. Ask the Stylist chat for “3 outfits for a date” or “how can I improve my style?”.

Boundary: now, next, later, cut

Now In the proof of concept, as built
  • Real accounts from the start: sign-up and log-in (email + password), with profile, clothes, and outfits stored in a real database; a profile with a photo, a username and a city, a new password, and deleting the account
  • Upload clothes with AI background removal; the AI picks the type and up to three colors, and turns away photos that aren't one piece
  • An item card to correct and enrich each piece
  • Outfit canvas for tops, bottoms, and shoes, starting from a random combination, plus layers: under layer, outerwear, bag, accessories
  • AI outfit generation from your own items, right on the canvas: one click, plus an optional request, with follow-ups
  • A Stylist chat: outfits for an occasion, pieces from the closet, your style and how to improve it
  • Saved outfits, editing them, Wear it today and a calendar of what was worn
  • Current weather for your city: on the canvas, in the stylist's choices, in a cold/heat tip, and on a weather page
  • Search and filters for clothes and outfits
Next The next step: friends
  • Add friends: a private circle, never a public feed
  • Share outfits with friends and see the ones they share
  • Rate friends' outfits
  • At the end of every month: the top 3 outfits with the highest scores, and the friend who collected the most points, the most stylish of the month
Later Worth doing, just not now
  • The morning outfit reminder and a daily streak in the calendar
  • OutfitCHECK: a photo of yourself in the day's outfit, kept in the calendar and shared with friends
  • Themed collections of saved outfits (“office,” “date”)
  • The full Stylist chat: saved chat history, and trying outfits on your own photo
  • AI auto-tagging of material and season (type and colors are in the PoC)
  • Planning outfits for the days ahead with the forecast
  • Pitch extras: Group Vibes, Wardrobe Swap, Travel Mode, Cost-per-Wear, Avatar Mannequin
Cut Out of the demo, with reasons
  • “One month later” time skip: depends on the calendar history, social features, and fake data, and none of it proves the styling engine.
  • Social in the PoC: “it can be built later once the core styling engine works.” It is the next step. (Weather was cut too at first; it came back during the build.)
