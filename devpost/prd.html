Mixyn

Product requirements for an AI stylist that builds outfits from the clothes you actually own, for anyone who wants to shop less and wear more of what they have.

Scope said: “real accounts from the start” and “AI outfit generation.”
Now we know exactly what they mean: a real email-and-password account whose wardrobe lives in a database, an AI that fills in only category and color, a four-slot canvas you can swipe piece by piece, and a stylist that falls back gracefully instead of failing.

The journey

Screen by screen

Account & session

develops scope.md › The POC Boundary
What you see
  • Splash on every launch.
  • Welcome: logo, “Mixyn,” description, Create Account / Log In.
  • Create Account: Name, Email, Password, City.
  • Log In: Email + Password.
  • Welcome/success message, then the wardrobe.
  • Profile: name, email, city, and Log out.
States
  • Returning user: Splash → Wardrobe, skipping Welcome.
  • Wrong password or email already taken: a friendly error on the form.
  • assumptionNo connection: “Can't connect right now, try again.”
You'll know it works when
  • First launch shows Splash → Welcome.
  • Create Account saves a real user in the database, then shows the wardrobe.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Log out, then Log In with email + password, brings back the same clothes and outfits.
  • Each user sees only their own data.
  • Reopening skips Welcome.
  • City is stored with the user.

AI stylist (the “aha” beat)

develops scope.md › The Unique Kernel · What “Working” Looks Like
What you see
  • Optional hint field: “burgundy,” “this oversized shirt.”
  • Generate with AI replaces the canvas with a full look.
States
  • No match: toast “We couldn't find anything burgundy. Here is another stylish option!” and a look anyway.
  • Missing category: the same placeholder as the canvas.
  • assumptionMay include outerwear if you own some.
You'll know it works when
  • An empty hint produces a full look from your own items.
  • A color hint favors matching items; an item hint builds around that item.
  • An unmatched hint shows the toast plus a fallback look.
  • It never uses an item you don't own.

Boundary

Building What we're building
  • Splash, Welcome, and real Create Account / Log In (email + password) with a persistent session and Log out
  • Database storing users, clothes, and outfits, each tied to its owner
  • Wardrobe with Clothes and Outfits tabs, including the illustrated empty state
  • Gallery upload with real AI background removal
  • Item card with AI Category + Color (editable); optional Name, Tags, Season
  • Canvas: random start, per-piece swipe, outerwear layer, placeholders
  • AI stylist with optional hint and graceful fallback
  • Save → Outfits tab, persisting across sessions
  • Deferred tabs visible but disabled (“coming soon”); Profile has account info and Log out
  • Japandi styling
Deferred Not in this proof of concept
  • Password reset, email verification, Google/Apple sign-in
  • Live weather: city is stored for later
  • Hats and Bags: hats need weather; bags don't show off layering
  • Camera capture: gallery is enough for the demo
  • “Wear it today,” Calendar, streaks, Collections
  • OutfitCHECK, Friends, sharing, ratings, leaderboard
  • AI auto-filling Tags, Season, Name
Non-goals Things Mixyn won't do
  • No public feed or public profiles
  • No “one month later” time-skip demo
  • No shopping or recommending new clothes: you style only what you own
  • No production deployment requirement

Open questions
